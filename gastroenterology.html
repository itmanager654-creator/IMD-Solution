Trusted Gastroenterology Devices in Lahore

IntraMed Solution is a Lahore-based supplier of disposable gastroenterology devices, serving hospitals, endoscopy units and private clinics throughout Pakistan. Our gastro devices Lahore catalog covers the full range needed for ERCP, diagnostic endoscopy and therapeutic gastroenterology procedures, from guide wires and sphincterotomes to biopsy forceps and PEG feeding sets. Every device we supply is single-use and sterile, reducing cross-contamination risk and supporting consistent procedural outcomes.

ERCP, Biopsy & PEG Feeding Solutions

Our range includes hydrophilic ERCP guide wires, rotatable hemostatic clips, injector needles, grasping and biopsy forceps, arch-shaped sphincterotomes, needle knives, biliary balloon dilators, stone retrieval balloon catheters, hot polypectomy snares, and complete PEG feeding sets in both luxury and standard configurations. These devices are sourced to match the specifications endoscopists and gastroenterologists already rely on, so switching to IntraMed Solution does not mean changing your procedure.

  • ERCP guide wires, sphincterotomes and balloon dilators for interventional endoscopy
  • Disposable biopsy and grasping forceps for accurate tissue sampling
  • Complete PEG feeding sets for safe, long-term enteral nutrition
  • Cleaning brushes and hemostatic clips to support safe procedure workflows

As a dedicated gastroenterology devices supplier in Pakistan, we work directly with hospital procurement teams and individual consultants to keep the right devices in stock, with on-time delivery across Lahore and nationwide.
