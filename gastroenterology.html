What is the difference between ERCP and diagnostic endoscopy?

ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) devices are specialized therapeutic tools designed for interventional procedures, including sphincterotomes, balloon dilators, guide wires, and stone baskets. Diagnostic endoscopy uses visualization tools like biopsy forceps, grasping forceps, and brushes for tissue sampling and inspection. IntraMed Solution supplies both ranges for complete endoscopy workflows.

Are PEG feeding sets available in different sizes?

Yes. Our PEG (Percutaneous Endoscopic Gastrostomy) feeding sets come in multiple configurations — luxury 24 Fr and 20 Fr complete sets, as well as standard sets — to accommodate different patient anatomy, clinical preference, and institutional protocols.

What makes single-use disposable endoscopy devices better?

Single-use disposable devices eliminate cross-contamination risk, reduce sterilization costs, ensure consistent performance, and simplify inventory management. They are especially critical for ERCP and therapeutic procedures where device precision directly impacts patient outcomes.
