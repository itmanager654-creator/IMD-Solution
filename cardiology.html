What's the difference between diagnostic and interventional cardiology devices?

Diagnostic cardiology devices are used for imaging and assessment (guidewires, diagnostic catheters, contrast injectors). Interventional devices are therapeutic tools for procedures like angioplasty and stenting (balloon catheters, stents, atherectomy devices). IntraMed supplies both for complete cardiac care workflows.

Are guidewires available in different diameters and stiffness?

Yes. Our guidewire range includes multiple diameters (0.014", 0.018", 0.038") and stiffness profiles (floppy, standard, extra-stiff) to accommodate different lesion types, anatomies, and procedural needs — matched to your cardiology team's preferences and protocols.

Why use single-use disposable cardiac devices?

Single-use disposables eliminate cross-contamination risk, ensure consistent device performance, reduce sterilization costs, and simplify inventory. For cardiac procedures where device reliability directly impacts patient safety, disposables provide superior quality assurance.
