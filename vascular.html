What's the difference between peripheral and coronary vascular devices?

Peripheral vascular devices are designed for larger vessels in the extremities, abdomen, and viscera, while coronary devices are specialized for small coronary arteries. Peripheral devices typically have larger lumens and different construction than coronary devices. IntraMed specializes in peripheral vascular supplies.

Are guidewires available for different vascular access?

Yes. Our guidewire selection includes varieties for femoral access, brachial access, radial access, and various lesion types (floppy, standard, hydrophilic, stiff). We stock multiple diameters and lengths to match your intervention protocols.

Why are single-use vascular devices important?

Single-use disposable vascular devices eliminate cross-contamination risk, ensure consistent device performance and sterility, reduce processing costs, and simplify inventory management. For interventional procedures, device reliability directly impacts procedural success and patient safety.
