    Questions

    Frequently Asked About Surgical & Medical Devices in Pakistan

    Where can I buy surgical devices and medical equipment in Lahore?

    IntraMed Solution is a Lahore-based supplier of disposable surgical devices and medical equipment serving hospitals, surgical centers, clinics, and practitioners across Pakistan. We supply surgical devices for gastroenterology, cardiology, vascular, and general surgical procedures. Contact us for direct procurement, quotations, bulk orders, and credit arrangements.

    What disposable medical devices does IntraMed Solution supply?

    IntraMed Solution specializes in three core areas: gastroenterology surgical devices (ERCP guide wires, endoscopy equipment, biopsy forceps), cardiology devices (cath-lab equipment, interventional cardiac instruments), and peripheral vascular devices (interventional vascular equipment, access sheaths). All devices are sterile, single-use, and meet international quality standards.

    Why choose single-use disposable surgical devices?

    Single-use disposable surgical devices eliminate cross-contamination risk, ensure consistent performance, reduce sterilization costs, simplify inventory management, and support regulatory compliance. For surgical and interventional procedures where device reliability impacts patient outcomes, disposables provide superior quality assurance.

    How quickly can you deliver surgical devices to Lahore?

    We maintain ready stock of commonly used surgical devices and offer same-day or next-day delivery across Lahore, plus nationwide delivery within 2-3 business days. For urgent surgical device needs and bulk orders, we provide priority scheduling and dedicated logistics support.

    Are your surgical devices certified and authentic?

    Yes. All surgical devices supplied by IntraMed Solution are authentic, sterile, single-use products that meet international quality and regulatory standards. We source from trusted manufacturers and maintain full traceability for each batch of surgical devices.

    What payment and credit terms are available for surgical device procurement?

    We offer flexible payment options including cash, bank transfer, and credit arrangements for qualified hospital, surgical center, and clinic customers. Contact our team to discuss pricing, volume discounts on surgical devices, and terms tailored to your institution's needs.
